# Speed Learn research framework

## Canonical source and editorial boundary

[`SLEARN_ORIGINAL_VISION.md`](SLEARN_ORIGINAL_VISION.md) is the canonical,
verbatim source for the supplied *Speed Learn*, *Down and Back Up*, *A Field
for Brains*, *Universe-Perspective Path*, *Jump*, *Maze*, and *Closure*
manifesto. Do not silently polish, normalize, or treat its imaginative
language as a theorem. This document is an interpretation and claim-status
layer around that source; it does not replace it.

The product's organizing relation is:

```text
Perspective → Why → Goal → Learning → Insight → Action → Experience
            → Return → Perspective′
```

`Perspective′` is a successor perspective, not a requirement that the initial
perspective be replayed literally. A return can preserve a declared relation
while retaining route, orientation, residue, and a new question.

## Claim levels

Every new research sentence must carry one of these levels in its surrounding
heading, table row, or ledger entry. A claim may have more than one level only
when the parts are explicitly separated.

| Level | Meaning in this repository |
| --- | --- |
| **Product design** | Intended learner experience, interface, workflow, or safety requirement. |
| **Phenomenology** | A report or vocabulary for lived attention, learning, or experience; it is not a causal result. |
| **Metaphor** | An image that helps reasoning (field, ball, maze, triangle, hair, or jump) without asserting its literal ontology. |
| **Metaphysical hypothesis** | A contestable thesis about truth, mind, nature, spirit, or reality. It requires argument and cannot be promoted by implementation. |
| **Mathematical definition** | A precisely stated object, relation, interface, or predicate with declared assumptions. |
| **Proved theorem** | A theorem checked by the named Lean source, limited to its stated assumptions and carrier. |
| **Empirical/runtime result** | A reproducible observation from a named bounded run, fixture, or user study. A scripted result is not a general-world result. |
| **Open question** | A question, conjecture, missing bridge, or untested consequence. |

| Canonical section | Repository reading | Claim level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-hour intelligence upload | Provocation for what low-friction learning could change; no such technology is claimed. | Metaphor / open question |
| Speed Learn | AI helps a learner select what, why, how, and what to do next; it must preserve learner agency and reasons. Speed is reduction of unnecessary translation cost between a current perspective and an insight's generative structure, **not information compression**. | Product design |
| Perspective and Why | A revisable map connects perspective, value/belief, WHY, goal, lesson, project, and returned experience. | Product design |
| Down and Back Up | The downward/upward triangle describes decomposition, recentering, and return. Natural/spiritual cycles and the brain–spirit reading remain interpretations, not product measurements or physical laws. | Phenomenology / metaphor / metaphysical hypothesis |
| A Field for Brains | Multiple people can contribute inspectable insight paths and counter-readings. Field, feet, ball, and game language are interface metaphors, not a demonstrated social science model. | Product design / metaphor |
| Universe-Perspective Path | Zoom from lesson or practice to goal, then to a wider world relation; zoom back to resume an experience. Paths can show methods, questions, and alternatives rather than force a single curriculum. | Product design |
| The Jump | Statements about consciousness, balance points, spacetime, ball trajectories, 0–∞, and global hair are hypotheses or interpretive models unless supplied with an independently stated mathematical model and proof. | Metaphysical hypothesis / metaphor / open question |
| The Maze | A path/obstruction image for translation and learning. A topological maze exists as mathematics only after a carrier, line set, topology, and theorem are separately defined. | Metaphor / mathematical definition / open question |
| Closure | Truth-level closure means relation-relative recovery through an admitted translation and return. It does **not** mean literal endpoint equality, ordinary `0 = ∞`, a final perspective, or a privileged origin language. | Metaphysical hypothesis; selected mathematical definitions and theorems are separately scoped |

## Operational consequences

1. Learning material should make its current perspective, WHY, goal,
   explanation(s), question(s), action, evidence, and possible return visible.
2. The interface may propose paths, but it must not convert a recommendation,
   completion click, model confidence, or feed engagement into a learner's
   insight or truth admission.
3. Any proposed transformation-cost metric must state its carrier, units,
   invariance claim, and evaluation protocol. No universal scalar cost,
   intelligence-upload mechanism, or pedagogical efficacy result is currently
   established.
4. Formal and runtime work must retain the distinction in the table above.
   Lean proof establishes only its declared definition; a finite runtime proves
   only its recorded fixture; neither validates a metaphysical interpretation.
