# Slearn Source Provenance

## Purpose and handling

This is a privacy-preserving source audit for the Slearn design lineage. It
records dates, source roles, and short source-disciplined paraphrases needed
to distinguish direct project notes from later synthesis. It does not reproduce
private Drive documents, personal material, credentials, file identifiers, or
private links.

`Original/raw first-person note` is a **content classification**: the retained
text has rough first-person drafting, corrections, or direct design language.
It is not a claim that a document is literally handwritten, nor a standalone
claim of authorship.  The Drive creation/modified timestamps establish only
the available file chronology; they do not prove authorship or handwriting.

## Method and classification

On 2026-08-17, the accessible Google Drive was searched for the keyword
`slearn`. The search returned **17 files** and no continuation page. Each
returned file was read in its available text form, including the one stored
Office document. This is a content/provenance audit, not a claim about the
truth of a source's proposals or a forensic determination of its author.

- **Direct project note** means the inspected Slearn passage is rough,
  first-person, unstructured design language, or an explicit project
  specification. It can be used as historical design evidence.
- **Mixed conversation** means direct user prompts or passages occur alongside
  generated/chat material. Only the direct passages are historical evidence;
  generated replies are not.
- **Secondary synthesis** means a later, organized document that explicitly
  assembles earlier notes, transcripts, code, or reported results. It can
  explain lineage but cannot replace a direct source.
- **Generated/undifferentiated** means the file presents polished framework
  prose without a reliable source partition. It is excluded from primary-note
  evidence.
- **Search artifact** means `slearn` occurred in an index or word list, not in
  a substantive Slearn note.

The record does not assert that any item was literally handwritten or prove
who typed every passage. In particular, a direct-note classification is not an
"AI-free" or authorship claim.

## Continuity recorded by the sources

The sequence supports the following **historical design continuity**, not a
proof of any metaphysical or mathematical claim:

```text
WHY-first learning
  → perspectives and values
  → learner-chosen goal paths
  → lessons / projects / world relation
  → returned experience
  → updated perspective
```

Later raw notes reuse that chain in a more speculative closure vocabulary:

```text
perspective ball ↔ WHY/goal hair ↔ maze ↔ translation ↔ returned closure
```

The first chain is the product/design lineage.  The second is an interpretive
research direction.  The Lean modules in this repository establish only their
declared, frame-specific formal structures; they do not certify the historical
notes, a physical light cone, a maze topology, or a general theory of mind.

## Exclusions and classification boundary

Polished 2026 IVI and translational-completion documents are retained, where
useful, as **secondary synthesis**.  They may clarify or organize the earlier
design, but are not used here as original-note evidence.  Search-index or
word-list hits are excluded as false positives.  This record intentionally
does not infer handwriting, authorship, revision authors, or factual claims
from Drive text alone.

## Use in repository documentation

When a later document draws on this lineage, label the statement as one of:

- **historical design provenance** — the limited chronology above;

- **interpretive research direction** — a hypothesis or vocabulary not proved
